CFSBR Technical Working Paper · Computational Methodology Standard

FinEngine: A Deterministic Computational Framework for Client-Side Financial Interfaces

Lead Author & Architect: Md Golam Mubasshir Rafi
Institutional Affiliation: Centre for Fintech & Strategic Business Research (CFSBR)
Publication Date: September 2026 · Version: v0.3.0
DOI: https://doi.org/10.67226/cfsbr.fe.2026.001.v1
Abstract: This document formalizes the deterministic computation rules, boundary constraints, and floating-point error mitigation heuristics behind FinEngine. It details localized financial engineering specifications for emerging markets—including reducing-balance amortization schedules, BDT sub-unit precision, and client-side debt burden validation for transparent fintech workflows.

Emerging-market roadmap modules

@finengine/risk

Credit risk and financial health analytics for lending, banking, and teaching surfaces.

@finengine/ratios

Financial ratio stacks and DuPont decomposition for statement analysis and classroom demos.

@finengine/microfinance

Microcredit transparency logic for flat-rate vs declining-balance interpretation.

Local-first assurance

All demo calculations run in-browser, which matters for teachers, researchers, and finance teams handling sensitive examples.

Bangladesh learning pathway

01 · BDT currency primitives

Sub-unit handling (Poisha), 3-2-2 grouping, cash in/out validation, and personal budgeting.

02 · Loan & EMI mechanics

Compound interest mechanics, amortized reducing balance, debt-burden ratios, and affordability scoring.

03 · MFS & merchant flow

Wallet settlement, ledger balance, fee reasoning, account mapping.

04 · SME cash flow

Sales, expense buckets, payable/receivable discipline, runway thinking.

A research centre working across financial technology, business strategy, and policy.

The Centre for Fintech and Strategic Business Research (CFSBR) works at the intersection of economics, computational engineering, business strategy, and law and policy. Its work connects research, publishing infrastructure, and practical programs for financial inclusion.

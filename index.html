Computation and reporting foundation

/core

validation kernel

Shared money types, rounding rules, ledger-safe validation, and deterministic domain contracts.

Without a hard validation core, lending, payment, and statement modules drift apart and audit explanations become weak.

/math

calculation engine

Amortization, XIRR, APR helpers, fee ladders, schedule generation, tax and payroll utilities.

/reporting

document outputs

Finance-oriented summaries, statement blocks, export schemas, and traceable audit-ready tables.

Prioritize consistency between on-screen previews and PDF/Excel export payloads, even before adding pro features.

Brand rule

Use self-made FinEngine marks only.

Do not mix in third-party product logos unless there is a real integration page and the trademark attribution is valid. The canonical mark on this site remains the FinEngine symbol you already approved.

Primary markTwo-tone FE symbol for site chrome and social cards.
WordmarkHorizontal lockup for banners, decks, and documentation headers.
Mono variantsFor single-ink print, embossing, and constrained dark/light use.

Usage guidance

These rules keep the mark readable, premium, and consistent across the website and documentation.

Clear space

Keep one mark-width of breathing room.

At minimum, leave padding equal to the width of the left "F" stem around the logo. Do not squeeze the mark into tight headers or buttons.

Minimum size

Use the icon at 24 px or larger.

For headers, 40–52 px works best. For favicons and app icons, use the dedicated exported PNG files instead of scaling screenshots.

Contrast

Match variant to background.

Use dark or original versions on light surfaces, light or mono-light versions on dark surfaces, and avoid placing the logo over noisy gradients.

Do
  • Use the original mark in the header and footer.
  • Use the wordmark for banners, presentations, and profile covers.
  • Use mono variants for single-color print or legal paperwork.
Don't
  • Do not recolor the canonical mark with random greens or reds.
  • Do not stretch, rotate, crop, or outline the logo.
  • Do not place third-party logos beside it unless the page is truly about an integration.

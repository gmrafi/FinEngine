About

FinEngine combines product clarity, open documentation, and research-backed finance framing.

The project is designed as a documentation-first JavaScript finance toolkit with a public-facing brand that can support packages, demos, and future enterprise extensions.

What the platform is trying to become

Open-source toolkit

Packages, docs, examples, and browser-local demos form the current public surface.

Enterprise-readable layer

The multi-page site improves how reviewers, teachers, and prospective collaborators assess maturity.

Research-backed framing

CFSBR context strengthens the institutional narrative without turning the homepage into a wall of copy.

Future extension path

Risk, ratios, microfinance, markets, tax, and corporate modules can be introduced gradually without bloating the front page.
